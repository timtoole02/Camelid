Continuation of `h74-legacy-lane-37-2026-08-25.md` on the same 16 GiB M4
Mini2. Baseline: the H74 promoted configuration
(`env/H74-legacy-compute-stack`, 37.31 tok/s mean). Target: 40. Every run
below is watchdog-qualified under the operator caps recorded in its receipt
manifest (`CAMELID_BENCH_MAX_CHILD_BYTES=11274289152`,
`CAMELID_BENCH_MAX_WIRED_BYTES=12079595520`); the non-overridable zero-swap,
swap-in-growth, pressure, and 2 GiB reclaimable-headroom guards were active
throughout, and every counted run is exact 48/48. Binaries:
`camelid v0.6.1-368-ge065271a` (probe sequence) and
`camelid v0.6.1-372-gb06da10b` (SHA-256 `4e9f47a7…`, all later sequences).
Mini2 was rebooted to zero swap before the first sequence.

## Systematic env-lever sweep

A three-way read-only audit of every `CAMELID_` switch consumed on the
gemma4 ghost-Metal/MTP path, cross-referenced against the promoted config,
produced exactly four untested candidates (everything else was either
already in the config, diagnostics-priced-at-zero, or a documented closure):
`HEAD_RESIDENT=1`, `KV_INIT=192`, a larger hot profile, and
`EXPERT_PACK_MIB=0`. All four were measured.

## H75a — resident head: DEFECT, fail-open

`CAMELID_GEMMA4_GHOST_METAL_HEAD_RESIDENT=1` (resident copy of the 605 MB
Q6_K head) produced **non-exact output and a mid-request 500** in both
probes (`mini2-h75-pabba-b1/b2`; watchdog receipts show zero memory
violations, so it is not an OOM). The harness exact-match gate refused both
runs. Filed as a defect: the opt-in must become raw-bit exact or fail
closed at load. Not a lever.

## H75b — KV_INIT=192: flat, and does not stack

Alone against controls: 37.56/37.55 vs 36.36/38.08/36.37 — inside noise.
Stacked on the pack-free winner (below): 37.50/37.97/37.28 (mean 37.58) vs
bracketing pack-free controls 38.33/38.26/37.37 (mean 37.99) — no gain.
Dropped.

## H75c — 2,400-slot capacity literal: closed

Commit `b06da10b` froze `GHOST_METAL_LEGACY_CAP_2400_HOT_PROFILE` (the H40
weighting scaled to the PROFILE_FREE ceiling) as a third admitted literal so
the live-sequential stage stays armed. The plain 2,400 profile was killed by
the reclaimable-headroom guard mid-request (1.917 GiB < 2 GiB floor) — the
+1.0 GiB slab is 0.23 GiB too greedy on this box. The lean variant
(+`KV_INIT=192` +`EXPERT_PACK_MIB=0`) ran clean at 37.92/37.21/38.23
(mean 37.79) against controls 37.82/37.12/37.29 (mean 37.41) — but the
pack-free-only arm at 2,100 slots in the same sequence measured **38.63**,
so the extra 300 slots are net **negative** once the pack cache is freed:
slab bytes cost the page cache more than hot slots earn. Capacity is closed
at 2,100.

## H75d — EXPERT_PACK_MIB=0: NOT promoted (drift-alias)

`CAMELID_GEMMA4_EXPERT_PACK_MIB=0` disables the per-MoE-layer CPU expert
pack cache (default cap 1,024 MiB/layer, filled during hybrid CPU prefill
and retained for the whole session). Its first two sequences looked like a
clear win (38.80/38.45 and 38.33/38.26/37.37 against 37.41-mean controls),
with the apparent saving spread across `slot_wait` rather than the demand
wave. The dedicated qualification A/B/B/A/B then reversed it:

| run | lane | tok/s |
|---|---|---:|
| A1 | H74 stack control | 37.94 |
| B1 | + EXPERT_PACK_MIB=0 | 36.84 |
| B2 | + EXPERT_PACK_MIB=0 | 37.44 |
| A2 | H74 stack control | 38.74 |
| B3 | + EXPERT_PACK_MIB=0 | 37.72 |

Pooled across every same-binary qualified run today: pack-free 37.90 mean
(n=8) versus control 37.78 mean (n=5) — +0.3%, inside run noise. The
earlier deltas were sequence-level machine-state drift, the exact aliasing
mode H73c documented. Not promoted; the switch remains available and
harmless.

## The honest population

Every qualified, exact run of the H74-stack family today (both binaries,
all sequences, n=13 control-class + n=8 pack-free) spans 36.36–38.74 tok/s
with a family mean of ~37.7 — statistically indistinguishable from the
promoted H74 checkpoint (37.31 mean). Single sequences routinely show
±1 tok/s coherent drift; nothing decided by fewer than ~6 interleaved
observations on this box should be believed.

## Where 40 stands

Not reached. From the promoted ~38 tok/s configuration, 40 needs another
~55–60 ms/request. The measured-lever space is now exhausted twice over:
this session's sweep plus the prior campaigns' H50–H74 closures. The
remaining walls are structural on this hardware and model: the ~226 ms
serial autoregressive assistant (bandwidth-bound, chain-dependent), the
~380 ms first-touch demand wave (prediction recall ceiling ~55–60%), and
the M4 GPU envelope on the verifier stages. Requantization (including
asymmetric formats) was considered and rejected: same-width asymmetric
formats are larger and slower on byte-bound walls, and lower-bit variants
change the model's outputs, forfeiting the exact-match contract this lane
is scored by.

## H76 — favorable-fixture exploration: 50 closed by measurement

Three new fixtures probed whether a speculative-friendly workload changes
the ceiling, each with its own greedy oracle captured twice (deterministic
in every case) and every spec run token-identical to greedy:

| fixture | schedule | tokens | tok/s | steady-state acceptance |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| TOML copy | K=15 | 128 | 35.4 | 11–14/15, syntax-token rejections |
| list renumber | K=15 | 90 | ~31 | mixed |
| pure line repetition | K=16 | 159 | 37.7 / 38.4 / 38.7 | 15/16, one miss per line |

Even maximally repetitive content plateaus at the same ~38: the round wall
grows with K (wider verifier passes plus longer serial assistant chains),
so (accepted+1)/round_wall is workload-insensitive at ~38–43, with 43 the
perfect-acceptance bound at measured K=16 round costs. A new fixture cannot
reach 50 on this hardware; the binding constraint is the round wall itself,
not content. All receipts under `mini2-h76-*`, watchdog-qualified with the
recorded caps.

## H77 — resident-head defect fixed and closed

The H75a defect is repaired (commit `73c00d97`): the MTP device chain
refused to borrow anything but the file-backed no-copy Q6_K token table, so
`HEAD_RESIDENT=1` failed generation mid-request. The chain now accepts
either backing (same aligned window, same buffer offset, raw-bit identical)
and a failed owned Metal allocation logs once and falls back to the
no-copy mapping. Verified on Mini2 with the previously-failing env:
36.65/37.88/37.17 tok/s, exact 48/48, zero violations, against interleaved
controls at 38.19/38.64. The opt-in is correct but not a throughput lever
(pinning 605 MB costs the page cache more than head refaults cost), so it
remains default-off.
