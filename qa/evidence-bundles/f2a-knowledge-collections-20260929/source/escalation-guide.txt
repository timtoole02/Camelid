Camelid Support Escalation Guide

Version 1.3, for support engineers. This guide says when a ticket leaves the support queue and who takes it next.

1. Outages

Escalate an outage that blocks every user in a workspace to the on-call engineer at once. Page the incident commander if the outage lasts longer than thirty minutes.

2. Security reports

Forward a suspected data breach to the security team within one hour of the report. Do not confirm or deny a breach to the customer until the security team has reviewed it.

3. Billing disputes

A billing dispute above 5,000 euros goes to the finance lead before any refund is promised.
